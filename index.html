About us:

Clarity Analytics is a Johannesburg-based South African company. Our core product is Canvas, a web-based data management and business intelligence platform.

Our approach

At Clarity Analytics we believe that data quality is at the heart of effective business decisions. Therefore our Canvas journey with you starts with your data. We work on setting you up with Canvas to extract, transform and integrate your data sources. Once your data is prepared, Canvas allows for the creation of simple, useful reports and powerful visualisations.

We recognise that the implementation of any new technology-driven tool can be daunting for users. It is important for us to support you through the initial implementation process until you are self-sufficient. Once you are trained and comfortable with our Canvas platform, you would be empowered with visibility of your data to create reports and dashboards that you can adapt as the needs of your business change.

The Canvas implementation cost, training, standard support and maintenance are not billed separately. We charge a simple, fixed and inclusive licence fee.

Our purpose

Our purpose is to enable your business to thrive from the value and insights in your data. We are an agile organisation driven by continuous improvement since 2017. We invite you to partner with us on a journey of growth and success.


Contact us at info@clarityanalytics.xyz for more detail or a free demo.

 
The business challenge landscape
Data pains
Sheer volume of data
Every business generates vast amounts of granular data. Is your data visible and easily accessible? Can you effectively package and utilize that data?
Data quality issues
Decision-making is often hindered by inconsistent, incomplete, or inaccurate data. Are you able to manage and enhance your data quality?
Siloed data
Siloed data can be a significant burden. Can you seamlessly integrate your various data sources?
Slow extraction
Waiting for information can be frustrating when you need it urgently. Do you wish you could extract your data more quickly to respond faster to business changes?
 
The business challenge landscape
Reporting and analysis pains
Inadequate reporting
Everyone encounters challenges with inadequate reporting - whether it's slow, manual, costly, inflexible, incomplete, or simply not useful. Are you able to access flexible, powerful, and self-service reports and KPIs in the format you need, right at your fingertips?
Lacking insights
With vast amounts of data available, understanding business levers, spotting exceptions and trends can be difficult. Can you easily overlay your business drivers and financial outcomes in simple, powerful dashboards?
Version control chaos
Updating reports and presentations often requires significant time and effort, and still result in version control issues. Do your reports feature automated, accurate updates with minimal manual effort?

Enhance data integrity
Get visibility of the quality of your data at the source. Tackle inconsistent and inaccurate data and derive meaningful insights, even from imperfect data. Maintain one version of the truth.
Remove the shackles of slow, manual and siloed reporting
Set up your reporting in the format that you choose, available as soon as the source is updated. Adapt, condense and expand the views depending on your needs and audience. Even integrate data from Excel if required.

Include multiple data sources into a single report/ dashboard or widget. Understand whether the key drivers of your business are generating the outcomes that you expect. Track these relationships over time to easily identify and quickly respond to changes.

Data discovery. Data quality. Data ownership. Data classification. Data summaries. Data pivots.
