About us

Clarity Analytics is a Johannesburg-based South African company. Our core product is Canvas, a web-based data management and business intelligence platform.

Our approach

At Clarity Analytics we believe that data quality is at the heart of effective business decisions. It is for this reason that our Canvas journey with you starts with your data. We work on setting you up with Canvas to extract, transform and integrate your data sources. Once your data is prepared, Canvas allows for the creation of simple, useful reports and powerful visualisations.

We recognize that the implementation of any new technology-driven tool can be daunting for users. It is important for us to support you through the initial implementation process until you are self-sufficient. Once you are trained and comfortable with our Canvas platform, we trust that you would be empowered with the visibility and understanding of your data to create reports and dashboards that you adapt and create as the needs of your business change.

The Canvas implementation, training, standard support and maintenance are not billed separately. We charge a simple, fixed licence fee.

Our purpose

Our purpose is to enable your business to thrive from the value and insights in your data. We are an agile organisation driven by continuous improvement since 2017. We invite you to partner with us on a journey of growth and success.

The business challenge landscape
 
 
Data management

• Every business generates vast amounts of granular data. Is your data visible and easily accessible? Can you effectively package and utilize that data?

• Decision-making is often hindered by inconsistent, incomplete, or inaccurate data. Are you able to manage and enhance your data quality?

• Waiting for information can be frustrating when you need it urgently. Do you wish you could obtain data-driven insights more quickly to respond faster to business changes?

• Siloed data can be a significant burden. Can you seamlessly integrate your various data sources?

Reporting and analysis

• Everyone encounters challenges with inadequate reporting—whether it's slow, manual, costly, inflexible, incomplete, or simply not useful. Are you able to access flexible, powerful, and self-service reports and KPIs in the format you need, right at your fingertips?

• With vast amounts of data available, understanding business levers, spotting exceptions and trends can be difficult. Can you easily overlay your business drivers and financial outcomes in simple, powerful dashboards?

• Updating reports and presentations often requires significant time and effort, leading to version control issues. Do your reports feature automated, accurate updates with minimal manual effort?
